# PR #250 round numbering — read this before trusting a directory name

The directory names are **not** his round numbers, and one exchange is split across two directories.
Both are historical accidents; the mapping below is authoritative.

| directory | contains | he calls it |
|---|---|---|
| [pr-round-2/](pr-round-2/) | his review of 2026-08-05 (`received/`) **and** our reply to it | Round 3 |
| [pr-round-3/](pr-round-3/) | his review of 2026-08-11 (`received/`), plus `PLAN.md` and `STATUS.md` | **Round 4** |
| [pr-round-4/](pr-round-4/) | our reply to that same 2026-08-11 review | — |

So `pr-round-3` and `pr-round-4` are two halves of one exchange: **his Round 4 review and our answer to
it.** `pr-round-2` keeps both halves together, which is the shape worth copying.

His review body opens by numbering itself ("Round 4. The three blocking items are fixed..."), so when he
refers to a round in conversation he means his number, not a directory.
