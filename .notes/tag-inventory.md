The **original** columns are write-once, recorded before any probe wrote to the tag.

`baseline` probe, so there is no per-block record to restore from.

**vs listing** compares what the seller claimed against what was measured. A mismatch is a
result, not an error -- an advertised count below the listing is the programmed-count case and
one above it is the phantom tail. Neither side gets corrected.
